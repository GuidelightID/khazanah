Al-Ma'tsurat is the morning and evening dzikir compiled by Imam Hasan Al-Banna.

## Caveat

The upstream repository does not state where its Indonesian translations come
from. Some items are Quran verses, whose Indonesian translation may follow the
Kemenag translation. If you redistribute this data in a context where that
matters, check the translation's source yourself. Corrections are welcome as
pull requests, with the source cited.
